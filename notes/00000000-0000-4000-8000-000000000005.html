Write mathematics

Inline math sits in a sentence, like x2+y2=1x^2+y^2=1x2+y2=1. Click it to see the dollar signs around its source.

Display math gets a row of its own. Click the equation below to reveal its source and the backslash-bracket markers around it:

a2+b2=c2 a^2+b^2=c^2 a2+b2=c2

Change a number and move the caret away to see it render. You can mix math with bold and italic text.
