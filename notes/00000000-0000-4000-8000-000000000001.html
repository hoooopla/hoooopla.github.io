Welcome to Math Note Editor. This is an example note: click this sentence and type. Your edits save automatically.

Markdown like syntax are also supported:

  • Bold
  • Italic

You may also use > at the start.

Start here

  1. Click + in the upright corner inside the toolbar to create a note. Its title is for reading; its label is its link address. Double-click the heading or press F2 to edit the title and label of the root block of current tab. Each block is saved as a markdown file in the workspace folder.
  2. Click Search (shortcut Cmd/Ctrl+K) to find a note. To link one while writing, type two opening square brackets and an autocomplete will popup.
  3. This embedded note starts open. Click its title to close and reopen it; click its content to edit that note without leaving this page:
    An embedded note you can edit

    This text belongs to a separate note. Click here and type something; the change stays with this note even if you close and reopen it.

    Try ↑ and ↓ to move across its boundary. With the caret on its title, Enter toggles it and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter opens it in a tab.

Images & workspace

Click in this note and press Cmd/Ctrl+I. The image picker lets you add an image or choose one from the assets folder; you can set its name, width, and description before inserting it. Cancel if you are only exploring.

Try Settings → General Setting → Manage Assets to browse images; renaming or moving one there updates its references. Notes are Markdown files in your workspace, and images live in its assets folder. Settings also contains Backup Recovery, shortcuts, math macros, and autocomplete.
